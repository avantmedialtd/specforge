## 7. Known follow-ups (deliberately not in this change)

Surfaced by the review passes, each needing scope or a decision this change does not own:

- **Footnote markers do not navigate.** `classifyHref` classifies every `#fragment` href as inert, so the `[^1]` marker and the `↩` back-reference render dimmed and non-clicking — more noticeable now that the footnotes section is styled as a first-class apparatus. Making them work means in-document anchor navigation plus a matching relaxation in the Rust `resolve_artifact_link` classification the frontend mirrors.
- **Scrollable blocks are pointer-only.** A wide table's `.table-scroll` (and a floor-held diagram's `.mermaid-block`) cannot be scrolled by keyboard: WebKit and Chromium do not make bare `overflow: auto` containers focusable. The standard remedy (`tabIndex={0}` + `role="region"`) conflicts with this codebase's documented choice not to grow a focus stop per document element, so it needs a spec decision rather than a quiet addition.
- **`overflow-y` computes to `auto` on the other wrappers.** `.table-scroll`, `.mermaid-block`, `.svg-block` and `.fence-block--error` declare only `overflow-x`, so a few pixels of vertical overflow could raise a spurious scrollbar, as it did for display math. Not simply mirrored from the math fix: `hidden` on a table wrapper would clip focus rings on links inside cells, so the right answer needs its own look.
