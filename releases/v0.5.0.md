SpecForge v0.5.0

Your dashboard gets a player: a personal profile, a monthly battle pass, and a home for everything you've shipped.

## ✨ Highlights

- **Developer profile** — SpecForge now knows who *you* are. It reads your git identity, lets you fold your various names and emails into one canonical "me," and gives you a locally-generated identicon avatar. Your streak, progress, and contributions are genuinely yours — teammates' history in a shared repo no longer inflates your numbers — and shared repositories gain a per-author leaderboard.
- **Seasonal battle pass** — a fresh monthly climb on top of your permanent record. Each season brings a generated name, a tier ladder, rotating objectives, and procedurally-generated badge finishes you unlock and collect in a locker. Standings reset each month so there's always a new climb; your lifetime work and streak never reset, and every season ends with an auto-written recap of what you shipped.
- **Archive browser** — browse and search your archived changes from a dedicated footer entrypoint, scoped per workspace, newest first. As a bonus, the archive is no longer re-parsed on every file change, so large workspaces stay snappy.

## 🔧 Improvements

- Season labels now count from OpenSpec's launch (September 2025 = Season 1), so the current month reads "Season 10" instead of a five-digit internal index.
- Inline `code` in spec text is now tinted, unboxed type — easier to spot when scanning dense prose, and clearly distinct from links.

## 🗑 Removed

- The **Milestones panel** is gone — it filled with retroactive trophies on first launch and then froze for months. Your equipped seasonal badge finish now lives on your profile avatar instead: a live mark that shifts as you climb.
- The Dashboard's **Me / Everyone** and **This Season / All Time** toggles are removed. The gamified views are first-person by nature; the team and season angles already live in the leaderboard and the season home.

### Downloads

- **macOS** — `SpecForge_0.5.0_universal.dmg` (Apple Silicon + Intel). Unsigned build: on first launch, right-click ▸ **Open**, or clear the quarantine flag: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SpecForge.app`.
- **Windows** — `SpecForge_0.5.0_x64-setup.exe` (installer, recommended — ensures the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime) or `SpecForge_0.5.0_x64-portable.exe` (single file; uses the system WebView2 runtime, preinstalled on Windows 11 and maintained Windows 10 — use the installer if your machine lacks it).
- **Linux** — `.deb` or `.AppImage`.
