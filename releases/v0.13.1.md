SpecForge v0.13.1

The ChatGPT quota gauge introduced in v0.13.0 reported the inverse of your actual usage. This corrects it.

## 🩹 Fixes

- **The ChatGPT quota gauge showed the opposite of your real usage.** v0.13.0 treated each window's percentage in ChatGPT's usage response as the budget *remaining* and displayed its complement, on the strength of a single side-by-side comparison that appeared to show the field inverted. It was not: the endpoint reports budget **consumed**, exactly as the field's name says. So the gauge rendered `100 − actual` — and it failed worst precisely where the gauge earns its keep. A weekly window with 92% of the budget gone displayed as 8%: a calm green bar at the moment Codex was about to start refusing work. In the other direction, a barely-touched window showing 5% consumed rendered as 95% and went red, raising an alarm about a quota that was almost entirely intact. The reading is now taken verbatim again, so the ChatGPT gauge means what the Claude gauge beside it has always meant: budget spent. If you enabled the gauge on v0.13.0, treat every figure it showed you as inverted. Nothing else about the gauge changes — window labels (`5h`, `wk`), the green / orange / red thresholds, the exhausted-window reset countdown, the stale de-emphasis, and the server-derived time axis are all as they were, and the Claude gauge was never affected.

### Downloads

- **macOS** — `SpecForge_0.13.1_universal.dmg` (Apple Silicon + Intel).
  This build is **unsigned**: on first launch, right-click the app ▸ **Open** and
  confirm, or clear the quarantine flag:
  `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SpecForge.app`.
