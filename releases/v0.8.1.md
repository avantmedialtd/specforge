SpecForge v0.8.1

The Claude quota gauge learns to tell time, and the terminal client gets its own settings.

## ✨ Highlights

- **A time axis on the quota gauge.** Each quota bar now shows not just *how much* of the budget is spent but *where you are in the window*. The 5-hour bar is split into 5 hour segments and the weekly bar into 7 day segments, with a live **"now" marker** that advances through the window as the clock moves. Read against the fill, it turns "how much is left" into the more useful **"am I on pace?"** — budget spent (the fill) versus time elapsed (the marker). It's derived entirely from data already on hand (no new network calls), and when a window's reset time is unknown the bar falls back to its plain unsegmented form. Present in both the desktop sidebar gauge and the terminal UI's title bar.
- **A Settings screen in the terminal UI.** `specforge-tui` gains a sixth screen — press **`6`** — that toggles its two opt-in features straight from the keyboard: **gamification** (the Dashboard/Season/Garden surfaces) and the **Claude usage-quota** gauge. `j`/`k` move, `Space` flips, and each change persists immediately and takes effect live — gamification refetches the gamified screens, and disabling the quota gauge clears it at once. Terminal-only users over SSH or in tmux no longer need to open the desktop app to flip these.

## 🔧 Improvements

- On the desktop gauge, the new segment dividers were thinned to a hairline and the "now" marker given a stronger accent, so the time axis stays legible against the green → orange → red utilization fill.

### Downloads

- **macOS** — `SpecForge_0.8.1_universal.dmg` (Apple Silicon + Intel).
  This build is **unsigned**: on first launch, right-click the app ▸ **Open** and
  confirm, or clear the quarantine flag:
  `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SpecForge.app`.
- **Windows** — `SpecForge_0.8.1_x64-setup.exe` (installer, recommended —
  ensures the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime) or
  `SpecForge_0.8.1_x64-portable.exe` (single file; uses the system WebView2
  runtime, preinstalled on Windows 11 and maintained Windows 10 — use the
  installer if your machine lacks it).
- **Linux** — `.deb` or `.AppImage`.

#### Terminal UI (`specforge-tui`)

`./specforge-tui`:

- **macOS** — `specforge-tui_0.8.1_macos-universal.tar.gz` (Apple Silicon +
