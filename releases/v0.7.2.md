SpecForge v0.7.2

Steadier season goals, and change rows that actually show their titles.

## 🩹 Fixes

- **Season goals stop climbing mid-month** — the battle-pass completion goal and its tier lines are now paced from your output *entering* the season and held fixed for its run, instead of rising as you ship into it. Your standing can no longer demote mid-season, generated objective thresholds stop creeping upward as you complete them, and a finished season's recap is graded against that season's own entry form rather than your current one.
- **Proposal titles reappear in the sidebar** — title extraction now skips blank lines, YAML frontmatter, and HTML comments before the heading and only accepts a real `# Title` h1. Changes on the newer OpenSpec spec-driven schema (whose proposal template opens with `## Why`) no longer show up as **"Why"** or fall back to their kebab-case IDs.

## 🔧 Improvements

- **Change rows show their proposal title** — git-singleton (worktree) rows and multi-instance change rows now label themselves with the parsed proposal title, falling back to the change name (still recoverable on hover) instead of always showing the kebab-case change ID.
