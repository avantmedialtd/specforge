SpecForge v0.1.0

A polish-and-presence release: SpecForge keeps your active-change count in front of you, lets you name and colour your workspaces, and reacts to edits the instant they land on disk.

## ✨ Highlights

- **Dock badge (macOS)** — your count of active changes across all registered workspaces now rides on the Dock icon and the ⌘-Tab switcher, visible even when the window is hidden or SpecForge is in the background.
- **Name and colour your workspaces** — give each registered workspace a custom display name and an identity tint, so you can tell apart look-alike folders and spot your favourites at a glance.
- **Completed task groups auto-collapse** — finished work folds away so your eye lands on what's still in progress; expand any done group on demand and the choice sticks.

## 🔧 Improvements

- Settings moved from a floating titlebar icon to the bottom of the sidebar, next to the workspaces it manages.
- Refreshed sidebar: flatter rows that read as a continuous list, the workspace tint condensed to a tidy 8px swatch with a hairline divider, a single predictable background on every platform, and quieter tree icons that drop the redundant "file exists" ticks.
- Cleaner empty state — workspaces with no active changes rest on their count badge instead of an extra "no active changes" row.

## 🩹 Fixes

- The workspace tree now updates immediately when you add or remove a workspace, instead of waiting for an unrelated file change or a restart.
- Task artifact state and progress counts refresh within the watcher's debounce window rather than lagging a beat behind your edits.
